2.1 The Diagnostic comprises a single, non-recurring written analysis of the Client's business (the "Services"), delivered in the form of a written report (the "Report").

2.2 The Services comprise:

    (a) review of the strategy documentation supplied by the Client;

    (d) independent use of the Client's product under conditions equivalent to those of an ordinary user;

    (e) in-depth assessment of the eight strategic pillars of the Client's business; and

    (f) delivery of the Report, identifying which of the eight pillars carry an open blind spot, which is to be closed first, and the order in which the remainder are to be addressed.
